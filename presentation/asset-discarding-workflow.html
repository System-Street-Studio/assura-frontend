Overview

Complete workflow for discarding assets from an organization

The Asset Discarding Workflow in the Assura system covers the full lifecycle of removing an asset from the organization's inventory. The process involves three key roles and ensures proper documentation, accountability, and transparency at every step.

Roles Involved

👷

Superintendent

Reviews and approves or rejects asset discard requests. Initiates the discarding process and provides the reason for discarding.

▼
🧾

Accountant

Confirms the discard after Superintendent's approval. Updates store records, uploads receipts, and creates official discard notes.

▼
📦

Storekeeper

Receives notification about discarded assets. Physically removes the asset from inventory storage.

Modules Covered

Module Purpose
Depreciation Calculator Calculates current asset value based on depreciation
Superintendent Dashboard Reviews, approves, or rejects discard requests
System Notifications Automatically informs Accountant after approval
Accountant Dashboard Confirms, documents, and finalizes discarding
Storekeeper Notification Alerts Storekeeper to remove asset from inventory

Depreciation Calculator

Determining the current value of an asset before discarding

Before an asset is discarded, it's essential to calculate its current value using depreciation. This helps the organization make informed decisions about whether to repair, sell, or discard the asset.

What is Depreciation?

Depreciation is the decrease in the value of an asset over time due to wear, tear, age, or obsolescence. The depreciation calculator in Assura computes how much value an asset has lost since it was purchased.

Example Calculation

Field Value
Asset Name Dell XPS 15 Laptop
Purchase Date January 2023
Value at Purchasing Rs. 150,000
Useful Life 5 years
Current Value Rs. 50,000
Total Depreciation Rs. 100,000 (67%)

Why It Matters

  • Helps decide whether to repair or discard — if repair cost exceeds current value, discarding is better
  • Provides financial justification for the discard decision
  • Maintains accurate asset valuation records for the organization
  • Required for audit compliance and financial reporting

Key Takeaway

"The Depreciation Calculator ensures that every discard decision is backed by financial data, not guesswork. It provides a clear picture of an asset's current worth to justify the discarding process."

Superintendent Dashboard

Discarding assets from the organization

The Superintendent is the first point of action in the discarding process. They review asset discard requests, verify the asset's condition and depreciation value, and decide whether to approve or reject the discard.

Dashboard Features

Feature Description
Summary Cards Shows counts for Pending, Discarded, Unread, Rejected, and Approved items
Clickable Filters Click any summary card to filter the queue by that status
Queue List Scrollable list of all asset requests with status badges
Detail Panel Full asset information — Division, Date, Time, Asset Type, Special Note
Review Flow 3-step process: Review → Choose Action → Add Note & Submit

Review Flow — Step by Step

1
Select an asset from the queue Click on any pending item to view its full details
2
Click "Review" Opens the action selection — Mark as Done or Reject
3
Choose an action "Mark as Done" (approve) or "Reject" the discard request
4
Add a note Write the reason for approving or rejecting
5
Submit Success card appears — "Approved & Submitted" or "Rejected & Submitted"

Key Takeaway

"The Superintendent reviews asset discard requests with full visibility into the asset's details and depreciation value. After review, they can approve or reject with documented reasons, ensuring accountability."

3

Inform Accountant via System

Automatic notification after Superintendent's approval

Once the Superintendent approves a discard request, the system automatically notifies the Accountant. There is no need for manual communication — emails, phone calls, or physical documents. The system handles everything digitally.

How It Works

1
Superintendent approves the discard Clicks "Approve & Submit" with a note
2
System processes the approval Asset status changes from "Pending" to "Approved"
3
Accountant is notified automatically The approved item appears in the Accountant's dashboard queue
4
Item appears as "Pending (Waiting for Confirmation)" Ready for the Accountant to review and confirm

Benefits of System-Based Notification

  • No delays — notification is instant, no waiting for emails or messages
  • No miscommunication — all details are passed automatically with full context
  • Audit trail — every notification is logged with timestamps
  • Paperless process — reduces physical document handling
  • Accountability — system tracks who approved, when, and why

Key Takeaway

"The system acts as the bridge between roles. No manual communication is needed — once the Superintendent approves, the Accountant is immediately informed through the system, and the item appears in their dashboard queue."

4

Accountant Dashboard

Update stores after Superintendent's confirmation about discarded assets

The Accountant is responsible for finalizing the discarding process. After receiving notification from the system, they verify the Superintendent's approval, confirm the discard, upload receipts, and update the store records.

Dashboard Pages

Page Purpose
Overview Queue of items with counter cards — Approved, Pending, Reject, To be Approved. Click items to view details and confirm discarding.
My Assets View all assets currently assigned or managed by the Accountant.
Discarded Table of all finalized discarded items. Click any row to see full details — Division, Date, Asset Type, Values, Special Note.
Receipt Upload and manage receipts for discarded assets as proof of action.
Discard Note Official discard notes with status badges (Approved / Pending). Click "View Note" for full details.
Lose Track lost assets — Reported, Under Investigation, Confirmed Lost.

Confirm Discarding Flow

1
Accountant opens Overview Sees approved items pending confirmation
2
Selects an item and clicks "Confirm Discarding" A confirmation modal appears with all asset details
3
Reviews details and uploads receipt Verifies asset information and attaches proof
4
Clicks "Save as Discarded" Item is finalized and moved to Discarded page
5
Success card appears — "Saved As Discarded" Discard Note is created, store records updated, Storekeeper notified

Key Takeaway

"The Accountant verifies the Superintendent's approval, updates the store records by confirming the discard, and keeps a proper paper trail with receipts and discard notes. Every action is documented for audit compliance."

5

Inform Storekeeper via System

Final step — notifying the Storekeeper about discarded assets

After the Accountant confirms the discard and updates the store records, the system automatically notifies the Storekeeper. This is the final step in the discarding workflow, closing the loop from identification to physical removal.

Notification Flow

1
Accountant saves the item as discarded Store records are updated in the system
2
System sends notification to Storekeeper Automatic alert with asset name, location, and discard details
3
Storekeeper removes asset from physical inventory Asset is physically removed from storage and marked as complete

What the Storekeeper Receives

  • Asset name and identification details
  • Division where the asset was located
  • Discard reason from the Superintendent's note
  • Accountant's confirmation details with timestamp
  • Receipt reference for documentation

Key Takeaway

"The Storekeeper is the final link in the chain. Upon receiving the system notification, they physically remove the asset from inventory. This closes the loop — from identification to removal, everything is tracked in the system."
